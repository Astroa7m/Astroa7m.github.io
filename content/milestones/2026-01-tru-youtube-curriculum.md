Thompson Rivers University asked permission to use my video "Fragments (Part 1)" from the
AstrosCoding channel in COMP2161 Mobile Application Development. I gave permission, and the
video is now required viewing for one of the course's learning objectives, starting with the
February 2026 intake. Students enrolled worldwide watch it on the course's Moodle, with a full
academic citation and a link back to my channel.

Their copyright team described the visuals and narration as very clear.
