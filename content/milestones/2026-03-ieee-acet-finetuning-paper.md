"Domain-Specific Fine-Tuning of LLMs for Multilingual Student Advisory in Higher Education".
A two-stage LoRA fine-tune for a student services assistant in English, Modern Standard Arabic,
Franco Arabic and Omani Arabic. Stage one teaches facts, stage two teaches it to refuse
out-of-scope questions safely. It beat GPT-4 on refusal accuracy (0.86 vs 0.82) and came close on
factual accuracy (0.78 vs 0.81), with the biggest gains in Omani Arabic.

Presented 21 to 22 November 2025, added to IEEE Xplore on 17 March 2026.
