Chapter 15, "Next-Generation Student Services: Developing an AI Assistant for Enhanced
University Support". It covers a real-time AI assistant that answers students' academic and
administrative questions around the clock, so staff can spend their time on harder cases.

The work came out of a project at Arab Open University, Muscat, funded by Oman's Ministry of
Higher Education, Research, and Innovation (Block Funding Program, BFP/UGP/ICT/24/001). We first
presented it at ICTIM 2025 (part of DIFCON 2025, Malaysia), where it won the Best Presenter award.
